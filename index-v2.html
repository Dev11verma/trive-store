CUSTOMER REVIEWS

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Our Heavyweight Hoodies are crafted in 380–420 GSM French Terry fleece and our Graphic Tees in 260 GSM combed cotton, both cut in a relaxed drop-shoulder oversized streetwear silhouette.
UPI Prepaid orders carry ₹0 shipping/handling fee and get priority dispatch. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on standard catalog items for an additional ₹149 handling fee. Custom Atelier pieces require UPI Prepaid.
Orders are dispatched within 24–48 business hours. You receive an email and live AWB tracking link (via courierupdates.com) as soon as your parcel is booked.
We offer a 7-day size exchange on unworn, unwashed standard items with original tags intact. Custom 1-of-1 Atelier orders are final sale unless damaged in transit.
OUR STORY

BUILT FROM CONVICTION, CRAFTED TO OUTLAST TRENDS

Born in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, TRIVÉ started with a simple refusal to settle for thin blanks and forgettable prints. Every drop blends sovereign Indian heritage, bold back-canvas storytelling, and heavyweight street tailoring under one creed: Born to Rule.

OUR VISION

ARCHIVAL STREETWEAR FOR THE FEARLESS

We produce in focused, quality-checked batches rather than mass fast-fashion runs—so every piece you wear carries distinct artwork, heavyweight drape, and lasting print integrity.
